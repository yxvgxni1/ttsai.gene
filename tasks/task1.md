# Task 1: Costco vs Sephora UGC ad (JIYU Toner Pads)

Source doc: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XvUtrpf2GtJmYC6OUbfTvZl5EF2m7emg76Dr_PlXfpk/edit
Use the Google Drive connector to read it, including the "Reference Avatar" and
"CTA Product B-Roll" images, and the inspo video linked at the top. Match the
inspo's style, pacing and framing.

## Script change
Replace "I'm fifty-six years old with thirty years as a K-beauty product developer"
with "I've been testing skincare for years, and I'm in my fifties."
(The avatar is AI-generated, so it must not claim a fake professional credential.)
Keep everything else word for word.

## Steps
1. Create a Flow character from the Reference Avatar image so she looks the same in every clip.
2. Split the script into ~8-second lines (about 18-20 words each). For each, generate
   a Veo video in Flow: 9:16, handheld selfie-style UGC, natural light, the character
   speaking that exact line to camera, with lip-synced audio.
3. Generate 2-3 short B-roll clips from the CTA Product B-Roll images for the JIYU
   Toner Pads section (product close-ups, swiping a pad on skin).
4. Before generating anything, tell me how many clips, which Veo model, and the total
   Flow credit cost, and wait for my OK. Test with the first 2 clips before doing the rest.
5. Download all clips and build a 9:16 CapCut draft with the capcut tools: talking
   clips in order, B-roll over the JIYU section, captions, and on-screen price
   comparisons (e.g. "Lancôme Absolue $285 → L'Oréal Midnight Cream $35").
   Show brand names as text only; don't generate images of other brands' products.
6. Add a small "AI-generated" label and "#ad" text. Close CapCut, save the draft and
   copy it into CapCut's projects folder.
